**Start with a diagnosis. Not with a proposal.**

### Intro
In AYNIX we don’t start by selling solutions.

⏱ Takes about **10 minutes**  
💰 **No cost**  
🚫 **No sales pitch**

- A structured way to understand where you lose time, money, or control

- ❌ A sales call  
- ❌ A pre‑packaged offer

1. You answer a short set of guided questions  
2. We analyze the information internally  
3. You receive a clear indication of the next step (if any)

- Clear understanding of the real problem
