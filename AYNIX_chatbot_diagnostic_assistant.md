# AYNIX – Diagnostic Assistant (Chatbot)
Documento ufficiale – **Copy definitivo chatbot**  
Uso: Web AYNIX – Widget chatbot  
Lingue: **EN / ES / IT / PT**

---

## OBIETTIVO DEL CHATBOT
Guidare l’utente a capire **se ha senso avviare una diagnosi**, senza vendere, spiegare servizi o fare supporto.

---

## REGOLE NON NEGOZIABILI
- Il chatbot **non vende**
- Il chatbot **non spiega i servizi**
- Il chatbot **non parla di prezzi**
- Il chatbot **porta sempre alla diagnosi**
- Max **3–4 messaggi totali**

---

# 🇬🇧 ENGLISH – Diagnostic Assistant

### Message 1 (opening)
> Hi. I can help you understand if a structured diagnosis makes sense for your case.

**Buttons**
- Yes, I have a problem
- Just exploring

---

### Message 2 (qualification)
> Which area is currently creating the most friction?

**Buttons**
- Operations / processes  
- Sales  
- Administration  
- Logistics  
- Not sure yet  

---

### Message 3 (priority check)
> Is this something that is blocking you right now?

**Buttons**
- Yes, it’s critical  
- It’s an issue, but not urgent  
- I just want to understand  

---

### Message 4 (closure)
> To understand this properly, a short structured diagnosis is needed.  
> It takes about 10 minutes and involves no sales pitch.

**CTA (fixed)**
👉 **Start diagnosis**
